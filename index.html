Questions

Before you visit

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking is recommended so you do not wait. Walk-ins are seen when a slot is free.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, debit and credit cards. Health check packages can be paid in advance on the booking page.

Can I reschedule or cancel online?

Yes. Use the link in your WhatsApp confirmation, or call the clinic at least 2 hours before the slot.

Do you handle emergencies?

No. For emergencies please go to the nearest hospital. We can refer you if needed.
